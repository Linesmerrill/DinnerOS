Salmon with Asparagus and Rice
Serves 4
Prep time: 10 minutes
Total time: 30 minutes
Ingredients
1 cup jasmine rice
4 salmon fillets
1 lb asparagus
2 Tbsp olive oil
2 cloves garlic
1 tsp salt
1/2 tsp black pepper
2 Tbsp butter
1 lemon
Steps
Bring the rice and 2 cups water to a boil, then cover and simmer on low for 15 minutes.
Heat the oven to 425°F. Snap the woody ends off the asparagus.
Toss the asparagus with 1 Tbsp of the oil and a pinch of salt on a sheet pan.
Pat the salmon dry, rub it with the rest of the oil, the garlic, salt and pepper, and set it on the pan.
Roast for 12 to 15 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily.
Melt the butter with the juice of half the lemon and spoon it over the salmon. Serve with the rice and lemon wedges.

Beef and Broccoli
Serves 4
Prep time: 15 minutes
Total time: 30 minutes
Ingredients
1 cup jasmine rice
1 lb flank steak
1 Tbsp cornstarch
1/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce
2 Tbsp brown sugar
1 Tbsp oyster sauce
1 tsp sesame oil
1 Tbsp vegetable oil
4 cups broccoli florets
3 cloves garlic
1 Tbsp fresh ginger
Steps
Bring the rice and 2 cups water to a boil, then cover and simmer on low for 15 minutes.
Slice the steak thin across the grain and toss it with the cornstarch.
Stir the soy sauce, brown sugar, oyster sauce, sesame oil and 1/4 cup water together.
Heat the oil in a large skillet over high heat. Sear the beef in one layer for 2 minutes a side, then take it out.
Add the broccoli and 2 Tbsp water, cover and steam for 3 minutes.
Add the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, return the beef, pour in the sauce and simmer 2 minutes until glossy. Serve over the rice.

Spaghetti Pomodoro
Serves 4
Prep time: 10 minutes
Total time: 30 minutes
Ingredients
12 oz spaghetti
3 Tbsp olive oil
4 cloves garlic
1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
1 can (28 oz) whole peeled tomatoes
1 cup cherry tomatoes
1 tsp salt
1 cup fresh basil leaves
1/2 cup grated Parmesan
Steps
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
Warm the oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and pepper flakes and cook for 1 minute.
Crush the canned tomatoes into the pan by hand, add the cherry tomatoes and salt, and simmer for 15 minutes.
Cook the spaghetti 1 minute less than the package says, and save 1/2 cup of the pasta water.
Toss the spaghetti in the sauce with a splash of pasta water for 1 minute.
Tear in the basil and top with the Parmesan.

Shredded Beef Tacos
Serves 4
Prep time: 20 minutes
Total time: 210 minutes
Ingredients
2 lb beef chuck roast
1 tsp salt
2 tsp chili powder
1 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp dried oregano
1 Tbsp vegetable oil
1 yellow onion
3 cloves garlic
1 cup beef broth
1 can (4 oz) diced green chiles
12 corn tortillas
1 white onion
1 bunch cilantro
2 limes
Steps
Heat the oven to 325°F. Cut the beef into 4 pieces and rub them with the salt, chili powder, cumin and oregano.
Brown the beef in the oil in a Dutch oven, about 3 minutes a side.
Add the sliced yellow onion, garlic, broth and green chiles. Cover and bake for 3 hours, until the beef pulls apart.
Shred the beef with two forks and stir it into the juices.
Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet.
Fill them with beef and top with chopped white onion, cilantro and a squeeze of lime.

Chicken Tikka Masala
Serves 4
Prep time: 15 minutes
Total time: 45 minutes
Ingredients
1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs
1/2 cup plain yogurt
2 tsp garam masala
1 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp ground turmeric
1 tsp salt
2 Tbsp butter
1 yellow onion
3 cloves garlic
1 Tbsp fresh ginger
1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce
3/4 cup heavy cream
1/4 cup fresh mint leaves
4 naan
Steps
Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces and stir in the yogurt, 1 tsp of the garam masala, the cumin, turmeric and salt.
Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the chicken for 5 minutes, then take it out.
Cook the diced onion for 5 minutes, then add the garlic, ginger and the rest of the garam masala for 1 minute.
Add the tomato sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
Stir in the cream and the chicken and simmer 10 minutes more, until the chicken is cooked through.
Top with mint and serve with warm naan.

Margherita Pizza
Serves 4
Prep time: 20 minutes
Total time: 35 minutes
Ingredients
1 lb pizza dough
1 cup crushed tomatoes
1 clove garlic
1/2 tsp salt
2 Tbsp olive oil
8 oz fresh mozzarella
1 cup fresh basil leaves
Steps
Heat the oven to 500°F with a baking sheet inside.
Stir the crushed tomatoes with the grated garlic, salt and 1 Tbsp of the oil.
Stretch the dough into a 12-inch round on a sheet of parchment.
Spread the sauce over the dough, leaving the edge bare, and tear the mozzarella over the top.
Slide the pizza on its parchment onto the hot sheet and bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until the crust is browned.
Top with the basil and the rest of the oil.

Chicken Skewers with Garlic Sauce
Serves 4
Prep time: 25 minutes
Total time: 45 minutes
Ingredients
1 1/2 lb boneless chicken breasts
3 Tbsp olive oil
2 lemons
4 cloves garlic
1 tsp paprika
1 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp salt
1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
4 flatbreads
1 cup dill pickles
1/2 cup green olives
Steps
Cut the chicken into 1 1/2-inch pieces and toss with 2 Tbsp of the oil, the juice of 1 lemon, 2 grated garlic cloves, the paprika, cumin and salt. Let it sit for 15 minutes.
For the garlic sauce, stir the yogurt with the rest of the garlic, a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt.
Thread the chicken onto skewers.
Grill or broil on high for 10 to 12 minutes, turning once, until cooked through.
Warm the flatbreads on the grill for 1 minute.
Serve the skewers with the flatbreads, garlic sauce, pickles, olives and lemon wedges.

Turkey and Bean Chili
Serves 4
Prep time: 15 minutes
Total time: 50 minutes
Ingredients
1 Tbsp olive oil
1 lb ground turkey
1 yellow onion
1 red bell pepper
3 cloves garlic
2 Tbsp chili powder
1 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp salt
1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
2 cans (15 oz) kidney beans
1 cup chicken broth
1 cup shredded cheddar
1/2 cup sour cream
3 green onions
Steps
Warm the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat and brown the turkey for 6 minutes, breaking it up.
Add the chopped onion and bell pepper and cook for 5 minutes.
Stir in the garlic, chili powder, cumin and salt for 1 minute.
Add the tomatoes, the drained beans and the broth, and simmer for 25 minutes, stirring now and then.
Serve topped with the cheddar, sour cream and sliced green onions.
