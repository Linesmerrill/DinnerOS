How a week goes

Sunday afternoon, five minutes, done. Then every night you already know what's for dinner.

  1. Plan

    DinnerOS fills the week with dinners your household likes. Don't feel like salmon on Thursday? Swap it in a tap.

  2. Shop

    The grocery list builds itself from the plan and skips what's already in your pantry. Send it to Walmart or a Kroger store, or shop with it on your phone.

  3. Cook

    Clear steps with timers you can tap, sized for however many people are eating tonight.

Built for a real kitchen

The small things that make it work on a Tuesday night.

One plan for the whole house

Everyone sees the same week and the same list. Invite your partner or a roommate, and whoever gets to the store first can shop.

It knows your busy nights

If you let it, DinnerOS checks how full your evenings are and saves the quick dinners for those nights. Your events stay on your phone.

Works with no signal

Your plan, recipes, and list are saved on your phone, so a dead zone in the store doesn't leave you guessing.

Your recipes too

Add a recipe from a website link or type in a family favorite. It goes into the plan like any other.
