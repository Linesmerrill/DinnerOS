Support

Something not working, or have an idea? We read every email.

Email us at

support@dinneros.app

We usually answer within two business days.

What helps us help you

  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if you can grab one.
  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The app version. In the app, open the Household tab, then Feedback & Bugs.

Please don't send passwords or card numbers. We'll never ask for them.

Using the beta?

You can also send feedback right from TestFlight: take a screenshot while DinnerOS is open, tap the screenshot, tap Done, then choose Share Beta Feedback.
