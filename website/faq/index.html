Questions

Can't find your answer? Write to support@dinneros.app.

What does DinnerOS cost?

Planning your week, grocery lists, cooking, and sending your list to a store are free. DinnerOS Plus costs $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, and starts with a 14-day free trial. Plus lets Autopilot plan the week for you and shows what each week costs.

What phones does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. There's no Android version right now.

How do I get the beta?

Install Apple's TestFlight app, then open the beta link on your iPhone. The beta is free, and new versions show up in TestFlight as they're ready.

Which stores can I send my list to?

Walmart, and Kroger stores like Kroger, Fry's, Ralphs, King Soopers, Smith's, Fred Meyer, QFC, and Dillons. Your list opens in the store's cart so you can check it and pick pickup or delivery there. You can also shop from the list on your phone or add it to Reminders.

Can my whole household use it?

Yes. Each person signs in on their own phone and joins your household from an invite. Everyone shares the same plan, recipes, pantry, and grocery list.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Your plan, recipes, and list are saved on your phone. Changes you make offline go through when you're back online.

What does DinnerOS do with my calendar?

Only if you turn it on, DinnerOS looks at how full each evening is so it can put quick dinners on busy nights. Just "free", "some", or "busy" and about how many minutes are free leave your phone. Your events never do.

What does it do with my location?

Your phone uses it to check the weather near you, so cozy dinners land on cold or rainy days, and to fill in your ZIP code when you look for a store. We only get the ZIP code and a rough weather label like "cold, rain". Your location never leaves your phone.

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. Share a recipe page to DinnerOS from Safari, paste a link, or type a recipe in yourself.

How do invite codes work?

Every household has a code in the Household tab. When a household that's new to Plus enters your code, you both get a free month of Plus. You can earn up to 12 free months this way.

How do I cancel Plus?

Apple handles the subscription. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and pick DinnerOS. Cancel at least a day before it renews. You keep Plus until the end of the time you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds go through Apple. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open the Household tab and tap Delete Account. That removes your account and your ratings. If you're the only person in your household, the household's plans, recipes, and pantry go too. If you pay for Plus, cancel it with Apple as well, since deleting your account doesn't stop Apple's billing.
